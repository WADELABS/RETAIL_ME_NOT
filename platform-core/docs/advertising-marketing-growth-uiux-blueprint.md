# 1. Strategic Lock

The business does not optimize advertising or interface decisions for impressions, clicks, conversion rate, gross sales, ROAS, or new-customer count in isolation.

The governing metric is:

```text
After-Marketing Contribution
= merchandise contribution
- advertising spend
- incremental discount cost
- channel fees
- returns
- cancellations
- fraud and chargeback losses
- incremental support burden
```

A campaign, feature, category, or geographic expansion is not successful unless it increases contribution without violating cash, fulfillment, trust, compliance, or service guardrails.

# 2. Advertising Architecture

## 2.1 Advertising Types

### Internal merchandising

- Owned inventory spotlight
- High-contribution placements
- Compatible accessories
- Bundles
- New arrivals
- Clearance
- Back-in-stock
- Upgrade paths
- Lower-cost alternatives

### Paid acquisition

- Search ads
- Shopping/product ads
- Retargeting
- Paid social
- Video
- Sponsorships
- Affiliate placements
- Referral incentives

### On-site sponsored placements

- Supplier-sponsored modules
- Brand-funded placements
- Co-op marketing
- Promoted product cards

All sponsored placements must be labeled and must pass relevance, stock, trust, and margin gates.

## 2.2 Campaign Eligibility

A product may enter a paid campaign only when product identity is exact, inventory is fresh, the listing is active, expected contribution is positive, allowable CAC is known, returns and chargebacks are inside limits, delivery is reliable, content rights are confirmed, MAP restrictions pass, and expected fulfillment obligations can be funded.

## 2.3 Advertising Profit Formula

```text
Allowable CAC
= first-order contribution
+ confidence-adjusted future contribution
- required safety margin
```

Future contribution is discounted by cohort maturity, attribution confidence, repeat-purchase probability, return rate, product lifecycle, and retention quality.

Initial controls:

```text
LTV_HAIRCUT=50%
MINIMUM_ATTRIBUTION_CONFIDENCE=70%
MAXIMUM_DAILY_BUDGET_INCREASE=10%
MAXIMUM_CHANNEL_SHARE=50%
EXPLORATION_BUDGET=10%
```

## 2.4 Campaign Decisions

```text
EXPLORATION
HOLD_AND_COLLECT_DATA
SCALE_MODESTLY
PAUSE_OR_REDUCE
ARCHIVED
```

Scaling is allowed only when data is mature, after-marketing contribution is positive, CAC is below allowable CAC, refund/cancellation/chargeback rates pass, attribution confidence passes, and fulfillment, support, inventory, and capital capacity pass.

## 2.5 Paid Search and Shopping

Priority:

1. Exact MPN
2. Exact model
3. High-intent product plus specification
4. Compatibility-specific query
5. Brand/category query
6. Broad discovery only after exact-intent profitability is proven

Use negative keywords for free, repair, manual, driver, torrent, employment, unsupported condition, unsupported geography, and irrelevant models.

Product feeds must match visible landing-page title, MPN, condition, availability, current price, shipping, return policy, and image.

## 2.6 Retargeting

Retargeting requires consent, current stock, valid price, frequency-cap eligibility, and no equivalent purchase. Stop after purchase, discontinuation, invalid price, opt-out, or campaign expiration.

Suggested cap:

```text
maximum 3 impressions per 24 hours
maximum 10 impressions per 7 days
```

## 2.7 On-Site Advertising Selection

```text
ad_score =
context_relevance
+ compatibility
+ expected_incremental_contribution
+ campaign_priority
+ inventory_confidence
- fatigue
- return_risk
- cancellation_risk
- layout_disruption
```

No fake alerts, hidden redirects, deceptive urgency, incompatible accessories, or ad-induced layout shift.

# 3. Marketing Roadmap

## Phase M0 — Measurement and Trust

Build campaign identifiers, first-party analytics, consent, contribution attribution, refund/chargeback attribution, cohorts, UTM governance, product-feed validation, policy pages, email authentication, and review collection.

## Phase M1 — Organic Demand Capture

Create exact-model pages, category guides, compatibility guides, comparisons, setup guides, new-release pages, authorized videos, structured data, and internal linking. Prioritize content by commercial intent and expected category contribution, not traffic alone.

## Phase M2 — Lifecycle Marketing

Required flows:

- Welcome
- Browse abandonment
- Cart abandonment
- Price drop
- Back in stock
- Post-purchase setup
- Delivery follow-up
- Review request
- Compatible accessory reminder
- Warranty reminder
- Win-back
- Business-customer follow-up

Stop messages after purchase when irrelevant. Suppress unresolved disputes and support cases.

## Phase M3 — High-Intent Paid Acquisition

Start with exact-model search, shopping, branded protection, compatible-accessory search, and controlled remarketing. Budget priority: owned inventory, proven distributor offers, high-margin accessories, proven bundles, then exploration.

## Phase M4 — Social and Video Validation

Use small tests for demonstrations, comparisons, setup transformations, creator partnerships, product education, and business credibility. Social must pass the same contribution gates as search.

## Phase M5 — Referral, Affiliate, and Partnerships

Build customer referrals, business and school purchasing, local partnerships, consultants, non-conflicting repair partners, content affiliates, and co-op marketing. Commissions are based on contribution and mature only after the return-risk window.

## Phase M6 — Brand Expansion

After predictable economics, add category campaigns, regional campaigns, B2B purchasing, education purchasing, premium support, bundles, and owned/exclusive products.

# 4. Channel Strategy

## Organic Search

Use model, category, comparison, compatibility, buying-guide, brand, deal, FAQ, and support-to-product pages. Avoid copied supplier copy as the only content, doorway pages, false inventory, and useless autogenerated pages.

## Email

Measure contribution per delivered email, incremental orders against holdout, unsubscribe, complaint, return, and support impact—not open rate alone.

## SMS

Reserve for delivery, pickup, back-in-stock, price-drop, fraud verification, and order exceptions. Avoid routine saturation.

## Social Organic

Use product truth, comparisons, setup demonstrations, compatibility education, releases, operations transparency, support competence, and Louisiana business credibility.

## Paid Social

Use retargeting, profitable-customer lookalikes, demonstrations, owned-product launches, and high-margin bundles. Reconcile platform reports to backend contribution.

## Affiliates

Require identifier, commission rule, category and brand restrictions, attribution window, fraud rules, return reversal, chargeback reversal, and prohibited-promotion rules.

# 6. Scaling and Growth Strategy

Growth requires trailing contribution, after-marketing margin, fulfillment SLA, cancellation/return/chargeback limits, support capacity, inventory freshness, cash coverage, site performance, and stable operating weeks.

Default caps:

```text
MAXIMUM_WEEKLY_REVENUE_GROWTH=10%
MAXIMUM_DAILY_CAMPAIGN_BUDGET_GROWTH=10%
```

Freeze growth when cash coverage, support, supplier acceptance, inventory freshness, returns, chargebacks, fulfillment, tax/compliance, or site performance deteriorate.

Expansion order:

1. Improve conversion on qualified traffic.
2. Increase compatible accessory attachment.
3. Improve repeat purchase.
4. Deepen proven categories.
5. Add a second supplier.
6. Increase profitable high-intent budgets.
7. Add adjacent categories.
8. Expand geography.
9. Add B2B and education.
10. Add owned/exclusive products.

Only one major growth vector should expand at a time.

# 7. UI/UX Strategy

## 7.1 Principles

1. Product truth before persuasion
2. Clarity before novelty
3. Compatibility before cross-sell
4. Contribution before raw conversion
5. Reversible experimentation
6. Mobile parity
7. Fast interaction
8. Accessible controls
9. Explicit system states
10. No dark patterns

## 7.2 Visual System

- Deep black and graphite
- White-gold or pale metal accent
- Neutral white surfaces
- Controlled chromatic reflection
- High-contrast technical typography
- Tabular numerals for prices/specs
- Large authorized media
- Minimal noise

Motion is 120–220 ms, reduced-motion aware, user-triggered, and never blocks checkout.

## 7.3 Responsive Breakpoints

```text
mobile: 320–767
tablet: 768–1023
desktop: 1024–1439
wide: 1440+
```

## 7.4 Header and Navigation

Desktop includes utility strip, logo, predictive search, categories, account, saved items, cart, and support. Mobile uses a compact search-first header. Search returns products, models, categories, brands, recent searches, guides, and compatibility matches.

## 7.5 Product Card

Required: image, brand, title, model, key specs, condition, price, stock, delivery, fulfillment disclosure, verified rating, promotion label, compare, save, and primary action. Stale inventory removes the active purchase action.

## 7.6 Homepage

1. Trust strip
2. Search/navigation
3. Hero
4. Owned-product spotlight
5. Personalized categories
6. Verified arrivals
7. Compatible bundles
8. Comparison entry
9. Buying guidance
10. Delivery/return/warranty trust
11. Sponsored module
12. Brand story
13. Support

Each module defines audience, eligibility, ranking, profit objective, fallback, frequency cap, impression, click, and contribution attribution.

## 7.7 Category

Sticky filters, active chips, relevance/price/delivery/performance sorts, comparison tray, truthful counts, in-stock toggle, spec filters, mobile drawer, and useful empty states.

## 7.8 Product Detail

Above fold: identity, model, condition, media, price, stock timestamp, variant, delivery, purchase, payment methods, return and warranty summary.

Below fold: specs, compatibility, included items, comparisons, reviews, setup, accessories, alternatives, questions, and support.

## 7.9 Cart

Show exact variant, current stock, price changes, fulfillment groups, delivery, compatible accessories, discounts, shipping, tax estimate, total, checkout, and save-for-later. No artificial countdowns.

## 7.10 Checkout

Contact, shipping, delivery, tax, Stripe Payment Element, final review, confirmation/pending. Require server totals, duplicate-submit protection, accessible errors, mobile keyboard correctness, and back-button safety.

## 7.11 Post-Purchase

Confirmation, pending payment, verification, fulfillment, split shipments, tracking, delivery, returns, warranty, support, and properly timed accessories.

## 7.12 Admin UX

Navigation: Dashboard, Catalog, Pricing, Campaigns, Growth, Orders, Payments, Risk, Fulfillment, Suppliers, Inventory, Returns, Tax, Compliance, Experiments, Audit, Settings.

Dashboard priority: loss/compliance alerts, capital, holds, profit exceptions, inventory exceptions, campaign exceptions, growth gate, then revenue and contribution.

## 7.13 Accessibility

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, labels, error summaries, screen-reader status, reduced motion, touch targets, zoom, accessible tables, captions, and transcripts.

## 7.14 Performance Budgets

```text
LCP <= 2.5 seconds
INP <= 200 ms
CLS <= 0.1
critical JS <= 200 KB compressed target
no ad-induced layout shift
```

# 12. Stop Conditions

Pause when after-marketing contribution is negative, CAC exceeds allowable CAC, cash coverage falls, chargebacks/returns rise, supplier rejection rises, support backlog exceeds capacity, inventory becomes stale, compliance is uncertain, performance degrades, misleading UX complaints appear, or promoted products lose eligibility.

Safe default:

```text
HOLD
PAUSE
REDUCE
SUPPRESS
REVIEW
```
